# DES Collaboration Agreements
## Co-Delivery Agreement Template + Job Plan Amendment Template

**For:** AI Launchpad operator (program manager) negotiating partnerships with DES providers
**Purpose:** Two ready-to-customise legal-document drafts that operationalise the AI Launchpad ↔ DES partnership: the **Co-Delivery Agreement** between the two providers, and the **Job Plan Amendment** that the DES provider lodges to substitute AI Launchpad activity for conventional job-search.

**⚠️ Legal disclaimer:** These are TEMPLATES, not legal advice. Have both reviewed by a solicitor experienced in disability sector partnerships AND by your insurer before signing.

---

## Template 1 — Co-Delivery Agreement

For AI Launchpad to provide specialist SLES delivery as approved DES activity to a partner DES provider's caseload.

### Cover sheet

```

Between:
    [DES PROVIDER LEGAL NAME] — "the DES Provider"
    ABN: [____________]
    Registered: [DEWR / DSS Provider Licence reference]
    Address: [____________]

And:
    [AI LAUNCHPAD LEGAL NAME] — "AI Launchpad"
    ABN: [____________]
    Registered: NDIS Provider Registration [____________]
    Address: [____________]

Initial Term:      12 months from Effective Date
Renewal:           Automatic 12-month rollover unless terminated per clause 9
Termination notice: 90 days
```

### 1. Purpose

AI Launchpad delivers a structured 2-year micro-business and self-employment program ("the AI Launchpad SLES Program") to NDIS participants aged 17-22 with disability. This Agreement enables the DES Provider to refer participants from their DES caseload to the AI Launchpad SLES Program, with AI Launchpad delivering on a co-delivery basis. The activity counts toward the participant's DES Job Plan as approved activity hours.

### 2. Definitions

- **Participant** — an NDIS participant who is also a DES Provider job seeker, referred to the AI Launchpad SLES Program under this Agreement.
- **Program Activity** — the AI Launchpad SLES Program structured weekly activity defined in the Weekly Delivery Rhythm specification (Schedule A).
- **Job Plan** — the DES Provider's signed agreement with the Participant under the relevant DES contract (legacy DES or SDEP).
- **Approved Activity Hours** — hours of Program Activity attributed to the Participant's mutual obligation per Centrelink reporting requirements.

### 3. Services AI Launchpad will provide

3.1 Deliver the AI Launchpad SLES Program to each Participant referred by the DES Provider, including:
- Weekly cohort workshop participation (2 hr)
- 1:1 support worker touchpoints (~0.5-1 hr/week per Participant)
- Structured self-paced module activity (auto-logged, 2-3 hr/week)
- Customer-facing and community engagement support
- Outcome capture and monthly snapshot reporting

3.2 Maintain a current NDIS Provider Registration in accordance with NDIS Practice Standards 2021.

3.3 Provide weekly activity logs to the DES Provider, in the format the DES Provider needs to report mutual obligation via ESS Web (legacy) or the relevant SDEP system. Logs include:
- Hours of Program Activity completed
- Hours of customer-facing / community activity (self-employment)
- Touchpoint attendance
- Cohort workshop attendance
- Self-paced activity hours (with idle-cutoff applied)
- Any Pattern D (reduced capacity) signals

3.4 Notify the DES Provider within 5 business days of any Participant safeguarding concern, reportable incident, or extended disengagement.

3.5 Maintain Public Liability Insurance ($20M), Professional Indemnity Insurance ($5M), and Workers Compensation as required.

### 4. Services the DES Provider will provide

4.1 Identify and refer eligible Participants from the DES Provider's caseload.

4.2 Negotiate the participant's Job Plan to include AI Launchpad SLES Program participation as approved activity, using the Job Plan Amendment Template (Schedule B) as the basis.

4.3 Report Participant mutual obligation activity to Centrelink using the activity logs supplied by AI Launchpad.

4.4 Manage the Participant's DES contract obligations (placement support, employer engagement where applicable, outcome tracking).

4.5 Notify AI Launchpad within 5 business days of any change to the Participant's DES status, Centrelink payment, or Job Plan obligations.

### 5. Co-delivery payment

5.1 The DES Provider pays AI Launchpad a co-delivery fee of:

```
$[300] per Participant per active week
```

Defined as: a week in which the Participant participated in at least 1 cohort workshop OR 1 in-person/sync touchpoint OR completed at least 4 hours of structured Program Activity.

5.2 Invoices issued monthly in arrears within 10 business days of month end.

5.3 Payment terms: 14 days net from invoice receipt.

5.4 Annual rate review at each renewal, indexed to the higher of CPI or NDIS Pricing Arrangements adjustment.

5.5 [Optional outcome share clause, suggested for renewal year 2 onward:]

> The DES Provider shall pay AI Launchpad an additional [25%-35%] of any 13-week or 26-week DES outcome payment that the DES Provider receives for a Participant referred under this Agreement, provided the Participant completed at least 6 months of Program Activity within the relevant outcome window.

### 6. Data and consent

6.1 Each Participant signs a separate consent for data sharing between AI Launchpad and the DES Provider, in addition to the four consents AI Launchpad obtains under its own Participant Consent Framework (Schedule C).

6.2 Data shared is limited to: activity logs, hours, touchpoint attendance, safeguarding flags, Job Plan-relevant outcomes. NOT shared: Participant's business income, customer details, personal medical information, AI Launchpad outcome reports for NDIA purposes.

6.3 Both parties hold data per Australian Privacy Principles, NDIS QSC standards, and DEWR data handling requirements as applicable.

6.4 Either party may request the other delete a withdrawn Participant's data within 30 days of withdrawal, subject to retention obligations under each party's own framework (NDIS: 7 years; DES: per contract).

### 7. Quality and compliance

7.1 Each party retains responsibility for its own regulatory framework — AI Launchpad for NDIS Practice Standards 2021, the DES Provider for NQSES and the relevant DES contract.

7.2 Both parties commit to quarterly partnership review meetings to surface issues, share outcome data, and refine co-delivery practice.

7.3 Either party may audit the other's relevant data and processes for Participants under this Agreement, with 14 days written notice.

### 8. Conflict of interest

8.1 Neither party shall make any payment, gift, or inducement to the other in respect of individual Participant referrals.

8.2 The Participant retains the right to choose, change, or refuse either provider at any time without penalty.

8.3 Either party shall disclose any actual or perceived conflict of interest in writing within 5 business days of becoming aware.

### 9. Term and termination

9.1 Initial Term: 12 months from Effective Date, auto-rollover for further 12-month terms.

9.2 Termination for convenience: either party may terminate on 90 days written notice.

9.3 Termination for cause: immediate written notice for material breach (including: loss of registration / licence, insolvency, criminal conviction, NDIS QSC banning, DEWR contract termination).

9.4 On termination, Participants currently in active Program Activity continue to be supported by AI Launchpad to the end of the current program quarter (3-month grace).

### 10. Limitation of liability + indemnities

10.1 Each party indemnifies the other against claims arising from its own negligence, breach, or non-compliance with its regulatory framework.

10.2 Neither party is liable to the other for indirect, consequential, or business-interruption losses.

10.3 Each party's total liability under this Agreement is capped at the fees paid in the preceding 12 months.

### 11. Dispute resolution

11.1 First step: written notice of dispute, response within 14 days.

11.2 Second step: senior management meeting within 30 days of notice.

11.3 Third step: mediation under the Resolution Institute Mediation Rules.

11.4 Fourth step: arbitration or court proceedings (Australian jurisdiction as applicable).

### 12. General

12.1 Australian law governs this Agreement. Jurisdiction: the State of [the AI Launchpad operator's home state].

12.2 This Agreement may be varied only in writing signed by both parties.

12.3 Notices in writing to the addresses noted on the cover sheet.

## Implementation notes

### When the Co-Delivery Agreement is signed

1. The DES Provider notifies AI Launchpad of the Participant's referral via the new tRPC endpoint `trpc.partnership.des.referParticipant.mutate({ ... })` (to be built).
2. AI Launchpad triggers the standard 7-stage intake flow.
3. On successful Stage 7 (formal enrolment), AI Launchpad notifies the DES Provider, who then drafts the Job Plan Amendment using Template 2.
4. The Job Plan Amendment is countersigned by all parties and lodged with the DES Provider's contract management system (ESS Web or SDEP).
5. Weekly activity reporting begins from Day 1 of Program Activity.

### When a Participant withdraws

1. AI Launchpad notifies the DES Provider within 5 business days (clause 4.5).
2. The DES Provider reviews the Job Plan and either:
   - Resumes the Participant's previous job-search obligations
   - Discusses an alternative pathway with the Participant
   - Records the withdrawal and works through DES Provider's standard exit process
3. AI Launchpad ceases new Program Activity but supports the Participant for the remainder of the current quarter (clause 9.4).

### Customisation per DES Provider partnership

Some clauses are likely to vary by partnership:

| Clause | Variable | Common range |
|---|---|---|
| 5.1 — Weekly fee | $200-600/participant/week | $300 is a defensible midpoint |
| 5.5 — Outcome share | 25-50% of 26-week outcome | 30-35% standard |
| 9.1 — Initial term | 6, 12, or 24 months | 12 months standard |
| 9.2 — Termination notice | 60, 90, or 120 days | 90 days standard |

The DES Provider's procurement team will negotiate. AI Launchpad's bottom line:
- Weekly fee not less than $250/participant (below this the SLES delivery doesn't sustain)
- Outcome share floor at 25% — anything lower devalues AI Launchpad's contribution to the outcome
- Termination notice ≥ 60 days — anything shorter is operationally disruptive

### NDIS billing during DES co-delivery

Important clarity: AI Launchpad bills the NDIS directly for the Participant's Capacity Building delivery, AND receives the DES co-delivery fee. Both are legitimate — they fund different aspects of the same delivery:

- **NDIS funding** pays for the Capacity Building component (the structured learning, the support worker time)
- **DES co-delivery fee** pays AI Launchpad for the additional scope: reporting to DES, attending DES case conferences, integration with DES outcome tracking, additional administrative load
- They are NOT duplicating the same service — each funds a distinct aspect of the same Participant's program

This dual revenue structure is what makes the SLES+DES partnership economically powerful: same Participant, two funding streams, no double-billing.

The Co-Delivery Agreement explicitly references this dual-funding structure in clause 5 to prevent any later allegation of duplication.

## What this template package does NOT do

- Does not constitute legal advice or replace independent legal counsel
- Does not auto-create the partnership — partnership begins after both signed signatures
- Does not commit AI Launchpad to a specific number of referrals — that comes through operational discussion separate from this Agreement
- Does not cover Aboriginal-Controlled Community Organisation (ACCO) DES provider variations or other specialist DES contract types — those have additional clauses (consult with the relevant cultural advisor)
