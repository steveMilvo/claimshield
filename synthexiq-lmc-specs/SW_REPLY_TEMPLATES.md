# Support Worker Reply Template Library
## 63 Templates — AI Launchpad LMC Touchpoints (Module 04B + 05–24)

**For:** AI Launchpad support workers handling 50 participants × 2.5 events/week = 125 billable events/week
**Purpose:** Quick-reply library so each touchpoint averages ~18 min (read learner work → pick template → 30–60s personalisation → send → bot closes billing event)
**Format:** Each template is a copy-paste body with `[BRACKET]` fields. Bot detects the SW's reply and closes the open `billing_events` row automatically.

---

## How to use this library

1. Open Telegram. See a 📋 *Billable event started* notification from the bot.
2. Look at the trigger phrase (e.g. `/submit_mod05_b`). That maps to **TP-05-B** in this library.
3. Read the learner's submission (screenshot, photo, or message).
4. Pick the right variant: **APPROVE**, **TWEAK**, or **REROUTE**.
5. Copy the template. Personalise the `[BRACKET]` fields. Send. Done — bot closes the event.

**Time budget:** SW should aim for an average of 8–12 minutes per touchpoint reply. Sync touchpoints (voice calls, group workshops, field visits) are longer by design.

**When NOT to use a template:** safety disclosures, crisis indicators, ambiguous learner work, anything that triggered the `crisis_events` pre-check. These need a real conversation, not a templated reply.

---

## Customisation rules (apply to every template)

1. Always start with the learner's first name. Never "Hi there" or "Hi participant".
2. Always pick ONE specific thing in their work to mention by name. Never generic ("good work").
3. Keep at Year 6 reading level. No jargon. No buzzwords.
4. End with the trigger for the next action (next touchpoint, next module, or "talk soon").
5. Voice notes (where listed) are 30–60 seconds — not 5 minutes. Brief beats polished.

## Escalation flags — do NOT use a template

Drop the library entirely and escalate to a real conversation (and your safeguarding policy) for ANY of:

- Crisis indicators (already pre-flagged in `crisis_events` table)
- Abuse, threats, or financial harm disclosed in a learner submission
- A learner stops responding for more than 7 days
- A learner consistently submits the same touchpoint after rejection
- Any health-related touchpoint where the learner describes a new severe symptom
- Any self-advocacy touchpoint involving children, court, or police
- Any reading-buddy submission that turns out to be a court summons, eviction notice, or debt collection letter

In all of these: voice call within 24 hours, document under your safeguarding policy, do NOT close the billing event with a template — log a manual SW intervention via the admin form.
